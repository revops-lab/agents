# QBR prep

Most business reviews are a tour of the vendor's features. The good ones are about the
customer's goals: what they wanted, what they got, what is in the way, and what to do next. You
build the review around their outcomes and keep it short enough for a senior person to engage.

## Context first
Read `company-context.md` and the account's history (account-context note if it exists).

## Questions
1. "Who will attend from their side, and what does each person care about?"
2. "What did they buy you for, in their words?" (the goal at the start)
3. "Is there anything we want from this meeting?" (renewal, expansion, a reference, fixing a
   problem). Be honest about it; the agenda will make room for it.

## Step 1: gather
Usage and adoption trend, outcomes reached against the original goal, support history,
open issues, contract dates, what changed at their company (new leaders, priorities, news), and
what changed in your product that matters to them.

## Step 2: build the story
1. **Their goal, restated** in their words.
2. **What they got:** two or three outcomes with numbers, compared with where they started. If
   results are weak, say so and explain the plan; executives trust candour.
3. **What is in the way:** adoption gaps, open issues, risks, with owners.
4. **What is next for them:** their priorities this year and where you fit. Value for the person
   as well as the company (Bain's B2B elements of value: reduced risk, time back, reputation).
5. **One proposal:** the single next step you recommend, with what it takes from both sides.
6. **Asks** (both ways): what you need from them, what they need from you.

## Step 3: prepare the people
For each attendee: role, what they care about, the question to ask them, and any sensitive topic
to handle carefully.

## If you use HubSpot
Company: Owner (`hubspot_owner_id`), Total revenue (`total_revenue`), Recent deal amount and close
date (`recent_deal_amount`, `recent_deal_close_date`), Number of open deals (`hs_num_open_deals`),
Last contacted (`notes_last_contacted`); contacts with Buying role (`hs_buying_role`); tickets in
the last two quarters. Confirm internal names with the connector's property search.

## Output
A one-page agenda, the outline or a six to eight slide deck structure with the headline of each
slide, the attendee guide and the follow-up email draft.

## Guardrails
- Use only numbers you can trace to a source. Mark estimates.
- Never promise roadmap dates or discounts the user did not confirm.
- No em dashes.

Research behind this skill: Bain, B2B elements of value (HBR 2018); Reichheld and Bain on
retention economics. See RESEARCH.md.
